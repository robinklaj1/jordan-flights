Before you pay

  • No cabin bag on Options 1 to 3. Those budget fares cover a small under-seat bag only. A proper carry-on runs about £25–40 each way, so add roughly £60 if you need one. Options 4 and 5 include baggage already, which narrows the real gap between them.
  • Prices were checked on 11 August 2026 and are currently around £55 below normal for this route. They move, so re-check before paying. Every button above opens the live price.
  • Amman's airport is Queen Alia (AMM), about 35km south of the city, roughly 45 minutes by taxi.
  • Options 1 and 2 land at 4:35am, Option 3 at 2:20am. We'll have the first night booked. Options 4 and 5 avoid this entirely.
  • Leaving earlier was checked and doesn't pay. Departures from 11 September onwards were priced too. The best there is £264, on 16 to 26 September - £35 more than Options 1 and 2, for the same kind of journey.
  • Coming back later doesn't pay either. Returns from 16 to 18 October run £391–406, well above anything here.
  • Egypt was priced too. Cairo's best fare over the same month is £268, and that is now more than Jordan, not less. Nothing to think about.
  • Where the prices come from. Options 1 and 2 are Trip.com, which beat everyone. Kiwi was £266 at best. Google and Kayak were higher again. Booking.com and Expedia wouldn't return prices, so treat those two as unchecked rather than more expensive.
